# Project Brief

## Project Name
Entering Research and Mentor Resources

## Purpose
Create a combined mentorship documentation system and website for Materials Advantage @ UCF.

The project has two connected parts:
1. Materials Advantage mentorship documentation.
2. Maahir's semiconductor mentor documentation and resources.

## Main audience
- Freshmen and sophomores who are interested but unsure.
- Transfer students trying to find direction.
- Students trying to enter research.
- Peer mentors.
- Materials Advantage officers.
- Students exploring semiconductors or other Materials Science fields.

## Main message
You do not need to have everything figured out. The point is to ask better questions, find useful resources, and take the next step.

## Research framing
Research should be framed as useful for any career pathway. It is not only for graduate school. It helps with industry, internships, professional confidence, technical communication, networking, and figuring out what kind of work students actually like.

## Semiconductor framing
The semiconductor guide is one example/backbone based on Maahir's UCF experience. It should help students explore semiconductors and understand how to break into semiconductor industry or academia through research, coursework, networking, conferences, and personal experience.

It should not make semiconductors sound like the only valuable Materials Science pathway.

## Removed content
Do not include medical school-specific modules.
Do not include high-school-student-specific modules.
Do not include source labels for the research-entry playbook.
