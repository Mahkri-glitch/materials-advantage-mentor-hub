# Materials Advantage Mentorship Documentation

## Purpose
This documentation helps Materials Advantage @ UCF create a simple mentor-mentee system that helps students enter research, explore Materials Science fields, and build career direction.

## What the program should do
- Help newer students understand where to start.
- Give mentors structure without making the program too formal.
- Make research feel less intimidating.
- Help students connect classes, labs, internships, conferences, and career pathways.
- Build a culture where students who receive guidance eventually give guidance.

## Core structure
1. Initial Mentor-Mentee Meeting
2. Mid-Semester Mentee Check-In
3. Mentor Philosophy Meeting
4. Mentor Office Hours
5. Research Entry Playbook
6. Pathway Resources

## Mentor philosophy
A mentor should listen first, give honest advice, help the mentee ask better questions, and encourage independence. A mentor should not try to make the mentee a copy of themselves.

## Office hours
Office hours should give students a low-pressure way to ask questions about research, professor emails, resumes, career direction, or technical pathways.
