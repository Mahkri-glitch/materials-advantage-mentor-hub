# Content Style Guide

## Voice
Write like a student who has actually been through the confusion and is trying to help the next student. Keep it direct, human, and practical.

## Good tone
- “You do not need to have everything figured out.”
- “Start by learning one process, one material system, or one measurement method.”
- “The goal is not to sound impressive. The goal is to show real curiosity.”
- “Do not mass-email professors. Pick a few labs that actually make sense.”

## Avoid
- Corporate training language.
- Overly polished AI tone.
- Medical school-specific content.
- High-school-student-specific content.
- Source labels in the research playbook.
- Making semiconductors sound like the only career path.

## Use simple headers
Prefer:
- Start From Zero
- Finding a Research Mentor
- Professor Email Builder
- What PIs Value
- Semiconductor Guide Backbone
- What I Learned in Banerjee Lab

Avoid:
- Synergistic student success enablement framework
- Comprehensive academic-professional alignment portal
